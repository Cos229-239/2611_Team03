Moodi App - Procrastination Feature

Team Member: Sheree Peterson
Project: Moodi - ADHD-Friendly Task Management App

My Contribution:

For our Moodi project, I worked on designing three screens in Figma that focus on helping users who struggle with procrastination and feeling overwhelmed.

Screen 1: Home and Encouragement

I designed a screen that welcomes users and provides encouragement from the Moodi mascot. The goal is to help users feel supported and motivated to begin their tasks.

Screen 2: Choose Your Task

I created a screen where users can choose what they need to work on, including school, work, household responsibilities, and self-care. I wanted the layout to be simple and easy to understand.

Screen 3: Task Progress

I designed a progress screen that helps users see what they have accomplished. I included visual progress indicators and check marks to encourage users to continue working toward their goals.

My Goal:

My goal is to make the procrastination feature easy to use, visually appealing, and helpful for people with ADHD. I also want my screens to flow consistently with the rest of the team’s designs.
